# button-group: ant-design → github-primer

Use GitHub Primer ButtonGroup as the primary inspiration because it treats a button group as a lightweight composition of related actions rather than conflating the pattern with an attached or segmented control. That distinction fits Monet better for product interfaces, toolbars, forms, tables, and data-heavy views where actions should read as related while retaining their individual semantics and hierarchy. Ant Design Space.Compact remains a useful reference for tightly attached controls, but its compact joined treatment is too specific to define the general Button Group pattern.
