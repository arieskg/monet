# text-input: none → ant-design

Use Ant Design's Input as the primary inspiration because its compact proportions, restrained border treatment, clear interaction states, and support for prefixes, suffixes, and validation fit Monet's dense modern product-interface direction. The field should remain visually quiet until interaction or validation requires emphasis, allowing labels and entered content to carry the hierarchy rather than heavy field chrome.
